My Career in Brief

I’m a product and organizational leader who builds teams that can navigate complexity with clarity and scale.

Over the past 15+ years, I’ve led product, design, and cross-functional organizations across early-stage startups, high-growth companies, and enterprise-scale businesses. Most of my work lives at the intersection of product strategy, organizational design, and the operating rhythms that help teams execute consistently well.

I’m especially drawn to products that improve everyday life—tools that support creativity, meaningful connection, and thoughtful consumer experiences.

How I Work

A few themes show up again and again in how I lead product and build organizations:

Clarity in Complexity

I help teams make sense of messy, cross-functional problems and turn them into clear, actionable priorities. That usually means framing trade-offs, sharpening strategy, and making sure everyone knows what we’re doing—and why.

Building High-Trust Teams

I focus on creating environments where people feel safe to speak up, take ownership, and do their best work. Trust, psychological safety, and honest communication are non-negotiable if you want teams to move quickly without burning out.

Systems & Operating Rhythms

I design lightweight systems—cadences, rituals, and decision frameworks—that keep organizations aligned as they scale. The goal is just enough structure to enable autonomy, not so much that it creates bureaucracy.

Thoughtful Product Strategy

I like to connect product decisions to the real needs of customers and the long-term health of the business. That means pairing qualitative insight with quantitative data, and being clear about what we’re betting on at each stage of growth.

My Career Journey

Foundations: Product, UX, and Engineering

Early in my career, I moved between product, UX, and engineering roles. That mix taught me how digital products are actually built day-to-day—and gave me a deep respect for the people doing the hands-on work.

Scaling at Enterprise: NOOK at Barnes & Noble

At Barnes & Noble, I worked on NOOK during a time when digital reading and e-books were still taking shape. I focused on content discovery and merchandising, learning how to design systems that serve millions of customers while still feeling human and curated.

Leading Product in High-Growth Environments

At Simple and ChowNow, I led product and cross-functional teams through periods of rapid growth. My work centered on product strategy, building leadership capacity, and putting in place the operating systems and culture that would let the organization scale without losing its soul.

Foundational Product Leadership & Advisory Work

More recently, I’ve been the first product leader at an early-stage startup and an advisor to founders and product leaders. In both modes, I’m focused on the same thing: building product organizations that have clarity of purpose, strong leaders, and the right systems to support thoughtful execution.
