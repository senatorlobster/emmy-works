About Me

How I became the product leader I am today

I'm a product and operations leader with over 20 years of experience solving complex, cross-functional problems — the kind that require deep listening, thoughtful planning, and a team of smart people pulling in the same direction. My work lives at the intersection of systems thinking and human-centered leadership: part creative problem solving, part organizational design.

I specialize in understanding the full landscape — business goals, customer needs, and technical constraints — and crafting a product vision that optimizes for all three. I design the conditions for products to get built by setting direction, testing assumptions, prioritizing outcomes, and ensuring everyone is aligned and empowered to do their best work.

Colleagues describe me as easy to talk to and refreshingly direct. I lead with thoughtfulness, clarity, and a focus on execution — while always staying open to new ideas and ways of thinking. I value transparency, clear communication, and healthy collaboration. And I'm serious about building teams that ship great work.

What I'm Looking For

  • A role where I can shape both product and culture
  • A remote-friendly team that values clear thinking, smart collaboration, and honest communication
  • A product with a proven business model, poised for thoughtful growth or evolution
  • A team that's low on ego, high on curiosity
❌

What I'm Not Looking For

  • Toxic culture disguised as "high performance"
  • Silos, politics, or a lack of clarity on how product contributes to the business
  • Teams that don't value product leadership or don't know what to do with it

If you think we'd work well together, reach out. I'd love to hear what you're building and why it matters.
