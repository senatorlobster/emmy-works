Let's Work Together

I'm open to hearing from companies building products I can believe in — especially those that bring joy, creativity, or meaningful utility into people's lives.

What I'm looking for:

  • A role where I can shape both product and culture.
  • A remote-friendly team that values clear thinking, smart collaboration, and honest communication.
  • A product with a proven business model, poised for thoughtful growth or evolution.
  • A team that's low on ego, high on curiosity.

What I'm not looking for:

  • Toxic culture disguised as "high performance."
  • Silos, politics, or a lack of clarity on how product contributes to the business.

If you think we'd work well together, reach out. I'd love to hear what you're building and why it matters.
